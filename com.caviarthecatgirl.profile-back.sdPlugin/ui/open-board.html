How boards work

Boards are profiles that belong to this plugin. They look and work like any other profile: add keys, pages and folders, rename them in Stream Deck's Preferences.

Unlike your normal profiles, a board can be opened from anywhere, and it knows how to get you back: Back returns to the normal profile you came from, Board Back to the previous board.

A board doesn't exist on your deck until you open it the first time. Stream Deck asks once whether to install it.

Stream Deck itself always starts on a normal profile. Applies to all keys.
