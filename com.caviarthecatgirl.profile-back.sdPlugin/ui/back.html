Back

Leave the boards

Takes you back to the normal profile you came from, like your game, no matter how many boards deep you are.

Where this key is
This key is on a normal profile. Back only works on boards: Stream Deck doesn't let plugins leave a normal profile. Pressing it here shows a warning triangle.
Tips

Put Back on every board, for example top-left. New boards already have one there.

Want to go back just one board instead? Use Board Back.

Play a sound and go back with one press: make a Multi Action with your sound key followed by Back.

Stream Deck itself always starts on a normal profile. Applies to all keys.
