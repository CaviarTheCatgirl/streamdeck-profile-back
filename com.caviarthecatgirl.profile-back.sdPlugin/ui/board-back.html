Board Back

One board back

Goes back to the previous board you opened. On the first board it works like Back and returns to your profile.

Where this key is
This key is on a normal profile. Board Back only works on boards. Pressing it here shows a warning triangle.
How the history works

Opening a board from another board adds a step. Opening one from your normal profile starts fresh. There is no limit to how deep it goes, and it survives restarts.

Pressing Back, or leaving the boards any other way, clears the history.

Stream Deck itself always starts on a normal profile. Applies to all keys.
